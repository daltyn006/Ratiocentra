## Industry

I could see the thin needles through the reflections of my tears. What was the reason of this brief respite before putting me under? Were they giving me time to prepare mentally with the newfound knowledge of the child in my womb, or the entirely dehumanized look they forced upon me. What will they do to me when I'm not conscious? The tears continued, I could feel them stick to my body as they fell to the ground, collecting in small preserved puddles at my restrained feet. Would the doctors take these tears as samples as well?

As If on que, a nurse with a syringe entered, soon followed by the woman who had took my, possible, last words from me. A rage burned with me "You bitch, you couldn't have waited a couple seconds to say goodbye to my love!?" "I'm sorry," she said in the same inhuman tone used at the front desk, "but since you have an infant we will need to alter your procedure slightly to accommodate it. Please verbally sign this waiver to allow this child to be trained as a doctor in your untimely termination." Only contempt rose in my heart- small embers flickered to pyre as the suggestion of my only child to become a worthless repetitive cog instead of the potential to explore the arts and rise to greater purposes than this scum. "Fuck you, no." "Your child will be terminated if you fail, good luck", she responded as if she were wishing the opposing team luck before a ball game, the same uncanny happiness scolding me for wanting anything unique for them. The nurse approached with the syringe, the tears that fell at my feet were taken just like whatever dignity I might have had. The embers burned the tears from my eyes and gave me foresight into the nurses and doctors as she left. They were tattooed in three separate places. One tatoo resided over their heart, another on their back, and a third one, in the small of their back- which was particularly unique- a large quick response code was stamped there.

A slow hum of electronics coming alive caused me to pass out. When I awoke, I was restrained in a private room- watched by at least 4 cameras. My limbs ached from being spread in the unnatural position, but the pain was comforting- it let me know I am alive, and not in the test. My head was not restrained, but I was being held in the air by seemingly nothing. I had restraints but they had no physical connection to anything, they must have been propelled by magnets? Or, maybe there was no gravity? These were the things they did not cover in the preparation, The room was hollow apart from one singular control table set directly behind me, a holographic model of me mirroring my position mocked my position. The conventional sound of mechanical movement slowly grew into focus irritating the room. Wait, It wasn't a model of me, it was me- down to the mole on my right shoulder, the only difference was this "me" had hair.  The wall in front of me illuminated, "Welcome! If you are watching this, you are having the privilege of becoming a rational being... " Who cares buddy. 

I continued to watch the room around me. white tiles, white drywall, white led lights, light blue tiles- oh! those are new. What if I look down? I'm not in a good position to see below me, diagonally facing up at a slant. Abyss. No, wait, the bright whites of the room just took a minute for my eyes to adjust--AH this is an uncomfortable position. Sometime between when I was trying to look down and when I was looking at myself the man on the screen stopped talking. "If I may have your attention?" a person was in the room? "Manada, if I may have your attention?" "Yeah, sure, what's up?" He motioned at the hologram, the room started to spin. holy shit, no human should rotate that fast. "The 'abyss' you were thinking about is the entrance and exit to your home for the year. If you succeed- you will leave in restraints, if you fail- your restraints will be released. Understand?" "Yeah, don't fail the test got it" "Get rid of that sarcasm or something might happen while your under" I'm not responding to that... "Alright, your test will begin in three minutes" "Wait- Isn't there mor -r -r -r-r"
