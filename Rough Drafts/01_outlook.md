# Part 1: Outlook

The pages turn, though, while the students listen — listen to the
professor drone on aforementioned knowledge, cumulative of the ages;

while the change from your pockets falls and glistens, giving way to
impertinent gleams.

Everything available to learn, hold true, and hear; the sanctity of a
degree — idol to all — causes fear;

When experience and thought fall through the fallacy, experts and
professionals weave facts making normalcy.

No information holds the mind gripping nor attractancy, till critical
thought comes unbound, unraveling;

Now prove your dependency, reliance, and fate; while proving
knowledgeable for the mentors distinguishing.

Right from wrong to exemplify perfection, waiting to hear about fate;

Hope for the future that funds the provision and wait to hear of your
rank.

Watch as the world corrupts education, to accommodate jobs that will
soon be forgotten;

Let others assemble worth with little proposition and figure you too
shall soon be begotten.

Turn to peers now all becoming rotten, as actions of rebellion show out
the shotten.

Causing pandemonious acts of rebellion, bringing progress to-the-ton.

Progress seen in places from decency to trepidation, as violence upon
violence expands across the nation;

From degenerate action slams the speculation, that what is truly popular
will worsen the condition.

All human lives rebelling to the easy, while what is truly good and
moral makes everyone quite queasy;

Bringing trouble to the colony, causing actions to seem matterease.

Progressing to find pleasure in drugs and in each other will not let us
surmise which person speaks the truth and who is the one that lies;

Continue to export thought and without effort to reflect, which progress
is beneficial, and which is neglect.

As leaves come to fall, and fall turns to winter, history churns round
yet still waits to bring cancer.

While those young and old both argue on decanter, falling on deaf ears
agreeing with no answer.

Progress from the past ideals causes people to turn to dust, as metals
in the ground wait patiently to aerate and rust;

Beams of steel fall with religion, being corrupted to fit the
formalities;

While heretics debate creation, and others debate philosophies — it
seeps into society and brings new favor.

Finding nothing but trouble and causing only more labor.

These loops must be forgotten, hopefully all too quick, else new idols
will rise up such as would a clique;

So be unafraid to act in ways ornate, lest you bring disillusion for
those who are praised as grotesque.

And find the future needs moderation, and to continue towards what
should be the statuesque.

Fail once more the process, in the name of what is best, instead of
trying methods which are proven unlike the rest.

Hold on to history and take no delays, to know which is written
propaganda and which to gaze;

Which stories bring fraternity, and which stories bring idolatry;

So, query in the present and always pause for the attention; that shall
bring a fall from revelations.

Bringing narcissism, violence, and the occasional peaceful protest,
leave free thought and cause a contest.

All in the name of progress.

Those willing to lead are not the best guides, for those knowledgeable
in the subject might bring a surprise;

The future will profit for those under disguise, unless there are
transparency and willingness to specialize.

For learning is not repetition and smarts won't take its place;

And knowledge is not given, but to have proof behind what one creates.

Finish all your studies and do not make a change, yet somehow still
contribute to the industry field's range;

Read allegorical novels, watch progressive musicals and movies;

Complete what has been deemed truth with no self-thought or example.

To please the past and present, neglecting the future, each person can
never be sure;

Unless the loop is broken, and a new-old age be awoken;

Through struggle and pain, with no blame;

Allowing awareness and listening to criticism from left and right,

Such that we might just all share our might.
