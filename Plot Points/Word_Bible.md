# World Bible & System Architecture

### 1. The Core Philosophy: Automated Bliss & Zero Friction
- **The World:** Following historical global collapse and extreme depopulation, surviving human society established "The System"—an automated, post-scarcity grid.
- **The Trade-Off:** Society traded risk, competition, heartbreak, and emotional suffering for guaranteed material ease, total comfort, and automated psychological stability.
- **The Population:** Extremely sparse. Physical human encounters are rare, making personal connections intense and high-stakes.

---

### 2. The Match System & The Absolute Law
- **Hyper-Optimized Matching:** Citizens opt into an automated matching algorithm that pairs compatible psychological profiles, eliminating rejection and marital friction.
- **The Absolute Boundary:** Infidelity, possessive abuse, or intentional psychological destruction of a partner is classified as a lethal social hazard. 
- **Real-Time Neural Monitoring:** The system monitors emotional/data projections. Boundary violations or severe infidelity trigger instant soul forfeiture and execution.

---

### 3. Subterranean Testing Mechanics & "Involuntary Sanctification"
- **Purpose:** Subterranean testing facilities evaluate citizens undergoing acute grief, attachment trauma, or social friction.
- **The Mechanics:** Subjects are placed in isolated chambers. Neural projections test their ability to "let go" of destructive attachments.
- **The Outcomes:**
  - *Serene Release:* Subjects who surrender their attachment experience lovely, painless peace and return to bliss society.
  - *Execution:* Subjects who choose possessive abuse/infidelity (e.g., Deneder) are eliminated cleanly.
  - *Self-Inflicted Nightmare:* Subjects who stubbornly cling (e.g., Grant) warp the chamber into an escalating, terrifying nightmare loop.
- **The Tragic Inversion:** The system offers gentle, merciful reform. Because human beings are stubbornly attached to their grief and pride, nobody chooses to reform—resulting in the passive, automated extinction of humanity.

---

### 4. Historical Archives & Grant's Cosmology
- **The Mall-School Library:** Physical paper records, obsolete books, and birth archives are preserved in an unmonitored side closet inside an old converted mall-school in Hasseoka.
- **Grant's "Unhoused Spirits" Belief:** Grant believes that citizens whose souls are wiped leave behind empty, wandering spirits trapped on Earth. He clings to his agonizing grief for Manada because he believes his pain acts as the sole vessel keeping her spirit alive.
- **The Pre-System Relic (The Bible / Book of Job):** In Chapter 24, Grant uncovers an ancient Bible. Reading the Book of Job confirms his belief that suffering and grief are the core of human integrity, cementing his final decision to reject society entirely.
