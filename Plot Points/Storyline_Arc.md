## SECTION 1: UTOPIA (Chapters 1–5)
- **Ch 1: Beskydarren:** Introduction to automated surface bliss, the Match System's zero-friction rules, and Grant's lingering grief over Manada.
- **Ch 2: Bodies in Downward Motion:** Contrast between contented system citizens and Grant’s spiraling paranoia.
- **Ch 3: Hasseoka:** Grant's father, background on society's sparse population and post-scarcity ease.
- **Ch 4: Vixen:** Deneder's possessive behavior established; Anderson and Christoph’s happy domestic pairings shown.
- **Ch 5: Iridium Necklaces:** Grant’s mental break over Manada’s loss. He takes a suicidal 10-meter leap down an industrial chute.

---

## SECTION 2: REDEMPTION (Chapters 6–10)
- **Ch 6: Lindesay:** Lindesay completes her subterranean transit test, mastering "letting go." Her platform ascends.
- **Ch 7: Revival:** Grant falls directly onto Lindesay’s ascending platform, stalling the lift weights. A nurse overrides the mechanism. Grant lies weeping on Lindesay; she comforts him.
- **Ch 8: Scattered Plans:** Quiet recovery. Lindesay and Grant bond over shared isolation and pre-system curiosities.
- **Ch 9: Lost and Found:** Exploration of Hasseoka. Grant shares his theory that failed souls become "unhoused spirits" trapped on Earth.
- **Ch 10: Archives and Data Centers:** Deep inside the Mall-School library closet, Grant uncovers physical paper files revealing his father's medical history and his lost twin sibling.

---

## SECTION 3: EXPLORATION (Chapters 11–15)
- **Ch 11: Exposure:** Grant refuses to use societies aides sets out into the Wild Grid. Husbands protect Grant; wives maintain marital bonds; Deneder seeks unmonitored freedom.
- **Ch 12: Fury and Hatred:** Domestic bliss perspectives contrasted against the wild journey. Deneder attempts to force affair tropes onto the wives and gets repulsed.
- **Ch 13: Resumption of Sanity:** Encounter with Grant’s 24yo sibling, spouse, and 5yo child. Sibling’s attachment shifts obsessively toward Grant upon learning of shared blood.
- **Ch 14: True Horror:** Grant breaks away, starving in the forest while holding onto grief. His family tracks him down to a surface restaurant, where he views their meal with disgust.
- **Ch 15: Knowledge Beyond:** Encroachment of the subterranean testing sector as the group enters the testing perimeter.

---

## SECTION 4: TESTING (Chapters 16–20)
- **Ch 16: The Glutton:** The 5-year-old child's early subterranean test, told through the surreal, terrifying lens of undeveloped child psychology.
- **Ch 17: The Tortured:** Deneder’s test. System projects his affair fantasy. He chooses possessive violation, triggering instant execution and soul forfeiture.
- **Ch 18: The Juggalo:** Sibling and group members undergo tests. Anderson and Christoph achieve peaceful release by letting go.
- **Ch 19: The Time Bomb:** Grant’s test begins. Mind-projection reveals Manada carried his child, which was clinically terminated due to parent friction.
- **Ch 20: The Misanthrope:** Grant violently rejects the test's offer of peace, choosing self-generated nightmare loops to keep his dead family's memory alive.

---

## SECTION 5: REALITY (Chapters 21–25)
- **Ch 21: Visionary:** Grant emerges from the testing depths alone, starved, ragged, and bleeding.
- **Ch 22: Consciousness in the Void:** The father's betrayal/mercy push. Lindesay's final attempt to reach Grant's soul.
- **Ch 23: Heat Rising:** Grant’s second, jagged chute descent down bare metal, leaving permanent physical scars.
- **Ch 24: The Fortress:** In pre-system ruins, Grant uncovers the pre-system Bible and reads the Book of Job, validating his belief that suffering is essential to humanity.
- **Ch 25: What Lies Beyond:** Grant walks into the cold wilderness lake accompanied by Manada’s phantom, choosing eternal illusion over surrender.
